❗ Questions & Answers Not Found on the Listing

Fixed knowledge you need to know — none of this is written on the property listing in Cirrus

Why this page exists A lot of guest questions can be answered by just reading the Cirrus listing. These can't — they're company-wide rules and policies that apply no matter what the listing says. If a guest asks one of these and you go looking for it in Cirrus, you won't find it there. Learn these instead.
Deposits & Payments
How long does the security deposit take to be released?
It's a temporary hold, not a charge. Released roughly 7–10 working days after the stay if there's no damage.
Can we get a discount?
No. The price shown is already our best rate, with no exceptions.
Can I split the payment across two cards?
No. That's controlled by the booking platform's own checkout, not something we can enable. Point the guest to the platform's support.
Can we pay with holiday vouchers?
Confirm the actual policy with Lauren before this goes live — not yet documented anywhere.
Pool & Dress Code (France)
Can I wear loose shorts or a wetsuit in the pool?
No. French law bans loose shorts and wetsuits at any French property pool, even if Cirrus doesn't mention it. Only a fitted swimsuit is allowed.
Are burkinis allowed in the pool?
No. Burkinis are never allowed at a French pool, no matter how fitted.
What We Never Share Before a Booking Is Confirmed
Can I give the front desk phone number before they've booked?
No. Only once the booking is confirmed.
Can I give the physical address before they've booked?
No. Only once confirmed.
Can I name a specific landmark when answering a distance question, before they've booked?
No. Keep it generic ("a few minutes' walk") — a named landmark can be reverse-searched to find the exact address. Once confirmed, be as specific as you like.
Can I tell them the property/hotel name before they've booked?
No, never — not even if the question sounds like it's coming from someone who already stayed. If the booking isn't actually confirmed, the answer is still no.
Can I confirm early check-in / late check-out / bed configuration before they've booked?
No. Defer it — "once confirmed, contact the front desk, based on availability, never guaranteed."
Contact & Communication Rules
Can I give a guest a V2 partner's direct phone or email?
No, never. We contact the partner ourselves — the guest never gets it directly.
Can I give a guest the Central Reservations number?
No. That's internal use only (French V1 resorts). The front desk number is the only guest-facing one.
Can I contact the guest, or be contacted, via WhatsApp?
No. The front desk isn't on WhatsApp — calls only.
Can I give out my own or Leavetown's direct contact details?
No. Platform policy means guests stay on the booking channel — we can't share direct contact info.
Can I move an OTA conversation off-platform (email, phone, an external link)?
No. It risks the whole account being suspended on that platform. Direct (non-OTA) bookings have more flexibility.
Can I point a guest to a partner's own direct-booking website?
No. That's for internal reference only — their calendar isn't synced with ours, so it can show availability that isn't actually real.
Should an AI draft repeat back the guest's own email or phone number?
No, never. It must be excluded from the draft — this is a privacy and platform-compliance requirement.
Service Animals & Accessibility
Are service animals allowed, and is there a fee?
Yes, always allowed, no pet fee — this is universal, not something that depends on the specific listing.
Is an emotional support animal the same as a service animal?
No. ESAs may not carry the same protections as task-trained service animals. Escalate unusual requests to Lauren rather than confirming it yourself.
The unit looks accessible (e.g. it's on the ground floor) — can I confirm that?
No. Never guess accessibility from general listing details. No explicit accessibility note anywhere in Cirrus means you treat the property as not accessible.
Photos & Units
A guest wants to see the exact room/bedroom they'll get — can the photos guarantee that?
No, not if there's more than one unit of that type. Always mention: units are individually decorated, photos are representative only, the exact room can't be guaranteed.
Can I confirm a specific floor or unit number in advance?
No. These are only allocated on arrival. A guest can note a preference via the front desk once confirmed, but it's never guaranteed.
What AI / Agents Should Never Decide Alone
Can I send an AI-drafted reply to a rude, upset, or refund-related message without review?
No, never. It always needs manual review first. Refund amounts, compensation, and policy exceptions are never decided in a draft.
Can I offer a discount or make up a policy answer on the spot?
No. Only fixed, pre-approved scripted answers — never improvised.
Airbnb Data Requests
An email arrives from "@airbnb.com" asking us to process user erasure requests — is it spam?
No, it's real. Find the guest's booking using the confirmation ID and delete their phone number (and email, if we have it) from Cirrus.

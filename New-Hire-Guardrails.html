🚧 Guardrails — What Not To Share / Do

Hard boundaries for guest & partner messaging · Read this before drafting any response

Why this page exists These are the things that don't just make a response wrong, they can cause real problems (privacy violations, platform suspensions, off-platform bookings, guests showing up somewhere with no reservation). Pulled together from Lauren's Slack threads, the AI project meeting notes, and the B2C training material in Notion — with the source noted under each rule so you know where it came from.
1. The booking-status gate
🔒
The single biggest guardrail: what you can share changes the moment a booking is confirmed
Before a booking is confirmed (Inquiry stage), a guest is a stranger who hasn't paid us anything yet. We do not hand over operational details that only make sense — or that we can stand behind — once there's an actual reservation. Once it's confirmed, most of these open up.
Detail📋 Inquiry (not booked)✅ Confirmed booking
Front desk phone numberNever "Once your booking is confirmed, you'll receive the front desk number and can contact them directly."OK Give the number from Cirrus.
Physical addressNever Not given pre-booking.OK Give the address from Cirrus (check-in address, which may differ from the resort's main address).
Named landmark near the property (distance questions)Never Keep it generic ("a few minutes' walk") — a specific landmark can be reverse-searched to find the exact address.OK Be as specific as Cirrus/search supports.
Early check-in / late check-out / bed configuration / crib requestsDeferred "Once confirmed, contact the front desk — based on availability, not guaranteed."OK Direct to front desk (V1) or coordinate internally (V2).
Property / hotel nameNever "We're unable to share the exact property/hotel name before your booking is confirmed."OK Always fine post-booking, including past-stay/checkout follow-ups.

2. Contact info & data privacy
🚫
Never give a guest the partner's/property's direct contact info for a V2 property
V2 properties have no front desk to refer guests to. We contact the partner ourselves in specific situations — the guest never gets the partner's number or email directly. All communication goes through Jetstream.
Source: Yurri training bot, Notion (B2C Guest Services), 2026-01-12 — "We never give the guest the partners contact information, we need to contact them ourselves."
📵
The Central Reservations number is internal only
For V1 French resorts, the Central Rez number is for the B2C team's own use for urgent internal issues. It is never given to guests — only the Front Desk number is guest-facing.
Source: Yurri training bot, Notion (B2C Guest Services), 2026-01-12.
🔐
AI drafts must exclude guest contact details
A guest's own email/phone should not be surfaced or repeated back in an AI-generated draft — this is a privacy and OTA-compliance requirement, not just a nice-to-have.
Source: AI Meetings Notes, Notion (Answering guest inquiries), October 2025 guardrail notes.
🇪🇺
Airbnb data erasure requests must be actioned, not ignored
If an email arrives from an "@airbnb.com" sender asking us to "process these user erasure requests," it's a real GDPR request, not spam. Find the guest's booking in Cirrus using the confirmation ID in the linked spreadsheet, and delete their phone number (and email, if we have it) from Cirrus.
Source: CHAT GPT MODEL - ALL B2C info, Notion (B2C Guest Services).
3. Off-platform contact, links & external sites
🔗
Don't send guests off-platform links or move communication off the booking platform
OTA bookings (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com) come in as inquiries and platform rules require keeping guest communication on that platform — "off-lining" contact with guests (email, phone, external links) is against platform policy and risks account suspension, which can affect the whole portfolio. Only direct (non-OTA) bookings give us more flexibility to follow up by phone/email.
Source: "Long stay bookings," Notion (CHAT GPT MODEL - ALL B2C info); "KR1: Platform Compliance" Q3 project doc, Notion, 2026-07-13 — zero platform suspensions is an active company KPI.
🌐
Don't refer guests to a partner's external/direct booking website
Partner direct-booking sites (e.g. a resort's own website) are for internal reference only, to fill gaps if our own Cirrus listing is missing info. Never use one to recommend a booking to a guest, check availability, or tell a guest to contact the partner directly — their calendar isn't synced with ours, so availability shown there may not match what we actually have.
Source: Coldwell partner guidelines, Notion (V1 Partners Overview), 2026-06-18. General principle — same logic applies beyond just Coldwell.
📧
Don't auto-answer emails or manually send messages that are already automated
Agents must manually respond to all email messages rather than using blanket auto-responses (Phase 1/2 guardrail). Separately, for VRBO specifically: do not manually send first-night check-in messages or review-request prompts — these already go out automatically, and sending them again duplicates the guest's experience.
Source: AI Meetings Notes, Notion, October 27 2025 guardrail notes; "SOP: VRBO Guest Messaging via Jetchat," Notion.
4. What AI drafts should never attempt alone
🙅
Refunds, complaints, and rude/upset guest messages always need a human
The AI can still generate a draft for a rude or upset guest message, but it must always get manual review before sending — never let it go out untouched. Refund amounts, compensation, and policy exceptions are not something to decide or promise in a draft.
Source: AI Meetings Notes, Notion, September 2 2025 and October 15/27 2025 guardrail notes.
💸
Don't generate offers, discounts, or freeform policy statements
Discounts, price reductions, and policy-based answers should come from the fixed rules Lauren has already approved (like the scripted answers in the Response Templates), not from the AI improvising in the moment.
Source: AI Meetings Notes, Notion, September 9 & 17 2025 guardrail notes.
🤖
Keep tone natural, not "ChatGPT-sounding"
Avoid excessive punctuation and overly formal, robotic phrasing. Responses should read like a helpful person wrote them, not a template. This is also why we track original message → draft → final response, so tone and quality can be reviewed over time.
Source: AI Meetings Notes, Notion, October 2025 guardrail notes.
5. V1 vs V2 partner rules
🏢
V1: partial access, has a front desk to refer to (once confirmed)
V1 partners give us partial access — we don't control their policies or calendars. We can refer guests to their front desk for early check-in, late check-out, or amenity/preference requests, but only once the booking is confirmed.
Source: Yurri/Sophie training bot, Notion, 2026-01/03.
🏘️
V2: we manage it, so we're the ones who act — not the guest
We fully manage V2 properties. There's no front desk to send a guest to — if something needs the partner (a real maintenance issue, a lockout), we contact them ourselves. During training, always check with a team member/team lead before reaching out to a V2 partner directly, since some are sensitive to repeated or unnecessary contact. Other V2-specific rules worth knowing: pets need a confirmed pet fee added to the booking (unless the property isn't pet-friendly at all, in which case just tell the guest no); no parties or large events at any resort; cancellations outside the free-cancellation window need a team lead's sign-off; age-restriction requirements must be met.
Source: Yurri/Sophie training bot, Notion, 2026-01/03; "V2 Partners Overview," Notion, 2026-07-28.
📨
Some partners have their own quirks — check partner notes before assuming the standard rule applies
Examples on file: IHG (Holiday Inn / Staybridge) doesn't answer emails — call the front desk instead. Banff wants on-site issues emailed to them directly rather than sent to their front desk. Always check the resort's Cirrus booking notes for anything partner-specific before defaulting to the general rule.
Source: CHAT GPT MODEL - ALL B2C info, Notion (Property Processes section).
